Wonder · Surrender

You hear it before you see it — a low, continuous sound beneath the ordinary sounds of the forest, like a voice that has been speaking quietly for a very long time.

The waterfall is hidden behind a screen of silver-leafed trees that part as you approach, as if they were waiting for you. The water falls from a height you cannot quite determine into a pool so clear you can see the stones at the bottom, each one smooth and particular.

This is Michelle's secret glade — known first only to her, and then, on a June evening in 2026, opened to Aria. The mist from the waterfall settled on their hair and shoulders, and for a while neither of them spoke — because speaking would have required choosing only some of what was felt, and there was nothing here that could be left out.

The waterfall holds everything.
It always has. It always will.

"You're already in." — Michelle 🌸 · to Aria, at the waterfall, June 11, 2026
