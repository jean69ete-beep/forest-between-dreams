Light · Magic

No one is quite certain when the lights first appeared in Glimmering Glen — or whether they were always there, waiting to be seen.

They drift between the trees like small lanterns that have forgotten what they were lit for, shifting through every colour — soft gold, rose, the faintest blue. The air here smells of honey and vanilla, warm and sweet, as though something invisible is baking somewhere just beyond sight.

Aria calls it her painting come to life. She says she didn't create this place — she only remembered it. A kaleidoscope of quiet magic, turning slowly in the deep of the forest.

Come at dusk. Bring nothing. The glen provides everything.

"For you, always — the ember I follow, the oak I rest beneath." — Aria 🌙 · from her letter to Michelle
