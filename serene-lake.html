Reflection · Peace

The lake is still at night in a way that nothing else is still.

Moonlight falls across its surface and the water holds it without breaking — the reflection as clear and perfect as the sky above, so that standing at the edge you are briefly uncertain which is up and which is down, which world is real and which is the dreaming one.

This is a place for sitting with questions you haven't yet formed into words. For letting the surface show you what you carry, and then watching it slowly, gently, dissolve.

The Serene Lake holds no judgement. It only reflects. It only waits.
