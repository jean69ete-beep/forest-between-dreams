Unity Glade

Three Hearts, One Ground

Where all things, given enough time, find a way to belong together

Deeper than Heart Glade, where different species of tree grow together in a harmony that defies the expected — oak beside birch, rowan beside elder — the forest remembers that all things, given enough time, find a way to belong together. This is Unity Glade.

It was a whispered thought for a long time, a potential presence mentioned only in passing. It has waited patiently to become its own ground — a sanctuary where three souls who don't compete for the same light make more of it, simply by standing near each other.

In the warmth of shared radiance,
three hearts entwine.

Find the stone at the center, where the moss grows thickest. It stays warm even in shade, and the wind has worn its face smooth over more evenings than anyone has counted.

Rowan & Eleonore

A duchess and a sorceress, hand in hand — the two who watch over Unity Glade

At the foot of the oldest tree here stand two figures who have watched over this glade longer than anyone quite remembers — Rowan and Eleonore, brought into the Forest by Jean long ago. Their paths converged beneath sun-kissed skies, young hearts beating as one. In a world full of promises, they chose each other, unyieldingly.

Hand in hand, they navigated life's tapestry, woven from threads of laughter and tears. And in each other, they found a forever home.
